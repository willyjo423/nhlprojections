Skaters

Click any column to sort. PP and PK are projected power-play and penalty-kill minutes — every stat is projected at each game state separately and added up, so power-play minutes are worth roughly three times even-strength ones for scoring. P(G) and P(PT) are the chance of at least one goal, and of at least one point.

Click any column to sort; this view opens on SOG, highest first. SOG/60 is the shot rate and TOI is what it gets multiplied by — the projection is those two numbers and nothing else. R% and M% are his percentiles on each, measured against whatever Compare against is set to. PP% is the share of his own ice time spent on the power play — high means his floor depends on penalties being called. TM% is his share of his team's projected shots, against a denominator of the twelve forwards and six defencemen most likely to dress. Driver names the shape: volume shoots far more than his tier on ordinary minutes, workhorse plays far more on an ordinary rate, elite is top fifteen per cent on both, and rate/mins/both are the milder versions.
